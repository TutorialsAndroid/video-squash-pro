VideoSquash Pro Desktop
Compressor About

Squeeze videos, not privacy

VideoSquash Pro is the offline, native FFmpeg desktop app that compresses videos of any size – 30 GB and beyond – without ever touching the internet.

🔒 Why it exists

Uploading private videos to online compressors is risky. VideoSquash uses the full power of your computer with native FFmpeg, streaming directly from disk. No upload, no account, zero tracking.

⚙️ How it works

🎬

Native FFmpeg

H.264, H.265, VP9, AV1 encoding. True multi‑pass quality.

📦

Batch processing

Queue up dozens of 30 GB files and compress them one by one.

🖥️

Fully offline

Once installed, no internet needed. Your videos stay on your drive.

🎛️

Full control

Presets, CRF slider, resolution, codec, container, and audio removal.

🛠️ Tech stack

  • Electron
  • FFmpeg (native binary)
  • ffprobe
  • Vanilla JavaScript
  • JSZip
  • CSS3
